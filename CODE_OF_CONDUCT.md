# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Puddle is a small, friendly project. We want participating in it — playing,
filing issues, contributing code, or hanging out in the community Discord — to be
a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, focusing on what's best for the project.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning conduct of any kind, in public or in
  private.
- Personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of
  discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository (issues, pull
requests, discussions), the community Discord, and any time someone is
representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to
the maintainers via the community Discord
([discord.gg/nH3dKXnN4u](https://discord.gg/nH3dKXnN4u)) — send a direct message to
a maintainer (`@rakdcolon`). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may
take any action they deem appropriate — including a warning, temporary
restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces — in response to behavior
that violates this Code of Conduct. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce it in
good faith may face consequences as determined by the project's leadership.
