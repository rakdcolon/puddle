# Volume II: The Mire

_Fourteen new puzzles, and this time they share a world._

Volume II is set in the mire, a single swamp with a recurring cast. A young frog
forever climbing out of somewhere. A patient heron taking its tithe. Dragonflies
racing the pond, fireflies waking on the reeds, mayflies changing hands, and an
old toad who counts in riddles.

Every puzzle is a beautiful classic re-skinned into that world, chosen so any
curious mind can crack it with a nudge or two, and built around a single elegant
turn. Two weeks of them, running daily from **June 28 to July 11** (Vol. II,
No. 26 to 39).

A few to look forward to:

- **The Frog in the Well.** Why the obvious answer is exactly one day too many.
- **The Heron's Tithe.** Fifty frogs in a ring, and the single safe pad.
- **The Foul Puddle.** Find one tainted puddle among a thousand, with ten newts
  and a single night before dawn.
- **The Line of Frogs.** Ten caps, one parity trick, nine guaranteed survivors.

The mire is wide, and this is only the first stretch of it.

---

_Volume II is the first themed collection. The vision: each volume is its own
world of intermingled, themed puzzles, about a month of them, each with its own
release notes._
