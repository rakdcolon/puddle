# Support

Thanks for using Puddle! Here's how to get help.

## Playing Puddle

Just head to **[solvepuddle.com](https://solvepuddle.com)** — a new puzzle every
day, no account required to play.

## Questions & community

The fastest way to reach us and other players is the **community Discord**:

**▸ [discord.gg/nH3dKXnN4u](https://discord.gg/nH3dKXnN4u)**

Good for: questions, puzzle discussion, feedback, and general chat.

## Bugs & feature requests

Found something broken, or have an idea? Open an issue:

- **[Report a bug](https://github.com/rakdcolon/puddle/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)**
- **[Request a feature](https://github.com/rakdcolon/puddle/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)**

Before filing, a quick search of [existing issues](https://github.com/rakdcolon/puddle/issues)
helps avoid duplicates.

## Security issues

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems. Follow the private
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Contributing

Interested in contributing code? Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
